One line of at most 200 characters: the phone battery's final message to the charger it waited for all day. A judge model reads lines two at a time and keeps the funnier one; the best-ranked lines take the top 8 places.

The battery waited all day by the outlet while the charger topped up everything else. Its last text lands the punchline in the final words:

> hey. it's me, 1%. waited all day by the outlet while you charged your laptop, your watch, your toothbrush — everything but me. don't reply, i'm already seeing the power bank.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (174 characters, one line).
